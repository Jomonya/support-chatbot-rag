Shipping Policy

We offer three shipping options:
- Standard shipping: 5-7 business days, $4.99 (free on orders over $50)
- Express shipping: 2-3 business days, $12.99
- Overnight shipping: 1 business day, $24.99

Orders placed before 2 PM EST ship the same business day. Orders placed after 2 PM ship the next business day.

We currently ship to the United States, Canada, and the United Kingdom. International shipping outside these regions is not yet supported.

Once your order ships, you'll receive a tracking number by email. Tracking can take up to 24 hours to update after the label is created.

If your package is marked as delivered but you haven't received it, please wait 24 hours (carriers sometimes mark packages early) and then contact support with your order number.
