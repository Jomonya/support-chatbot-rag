Returns and Refunds Policy

You may return most items within 30 days of delivery for a full refund, provided they are unused and in their original packaging.

To start a return:
1. Go to "Order History" in your account
2. Select the order and click "Start a Return"
3. Print the prepaid return label
4. Drop the package off at any carrier location

Refunds are processed within 5-7 business days after we receive the returned item. Refunds are issued to the original payment method.

Sale items marked "Final Sale" cannot be returned or exchanged.

Damaged or defective items: If an item arrives damaged or defective, contact support within 7 days of delivery with photos of the damage. We will send a replacement at no cost or issue a full refund, your choice.

Exchanges: We don't process direct exchanges. Please return the original item for a refund and place a new order for the item you want instead.
